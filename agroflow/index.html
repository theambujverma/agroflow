Farm to bottle

We skip the middlemen. You get the purity.

Most "cold-pressed" oil on the shelf has travelled through three or four traders before it reaches a bottling plant. AgroFlow works differently — our team buys mustard and coconut directly from growers, presses it within days of harvest, and bottles it without any refining, bleaching or deodorising.

That means the oil you get still has its natural pungency, colour and nutrients intact — exactly how oil used to taste before industrial refining took over.
