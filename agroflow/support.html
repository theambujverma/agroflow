Help & Support

Track an order, ask about returns, or raise a support ticket — our team picks these up daily.

Frequently asked

How long does delivery take? ⌄

Most orders are delivered within 3–6 business days depending on your pincode.

Can I pay cash on delivery? ⌄

Yes — choose "Cash on Delivery" at checkout. Online payment via Razorpay is also available.

What is your return policy? ⌄

If a bottle arrives damaged or leaking, raise a ticket below within 48 hours with your order number and we'll replace it free of cost.

Is the oil 100% pure? ⌄

Yes, all AgroFlow oils are unrefined, unblended and cold-pressed with no additives.
